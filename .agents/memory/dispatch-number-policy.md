---
name: Dispatch number policy
description: The shared field for LR and courier tracking numbers in B2B dispatch.
---

Both LR numbers and courier numbers must be updated in the LR number field only.

**Why:** The user explicitly clarified this B2B dispatch workflow.

**How to apply:** Keep one field labelled LR number that accepts either an LR number or a courier tracking number. Do not introduce a separate courier-number field.
