Paid leave, informed absences and half-days use available PTO first. Uninformed absences are always unpaid. Each uncovered full day deducts ₹500; an uncovered half-day deducts ₹250.

**Why:** The user explicitly confirmed these payroll rules, resolving details absent from the original design document.

**How to apply:** Use the fixed attendance deductions above, not the daily salary rate. The separately confirmed daily salary basis is (base salary + transport allowance) ÷ 30.

After the third weekend-adjacency violation in a calendar year, deduct ₹1,000 for each additional violation.

**Why:** The user confirmed a per-extra-violation penalty, rather than a one-off annual or recurring monthly charge.

**How to apply:** Reset the annual threshold at the calendar-year boundary; do not charge again for prior months' violations.
